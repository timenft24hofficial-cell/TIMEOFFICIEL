Frequently asked questions

What is Time?

Time is a collection of 1,440 NFTs on Solana, one for every minute of the day from 00:00:00 to 23:59:59. Each NFT is a 60-second video of its own minute.

How many NFTs will exist?

Exactly 1,440. A day cannot have more minutes, so the supply is fixed.

Which blockchain does it use?

Solana. Live interaction is planned through MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollups.

When does the mint open?

The date has not been announced yet. Follow our Discord and X for the official announcement.

What do I need to collect a minute?

A Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare, and a small amount of SOL for the mint price and network fees.

Can I choose my own minute?

How minutes are claimed will be explained before the mint opens. The aim is that every collector can own a minute that matters to them.

What are live minutes?

Planned features that let holders write a message to their minute and grow a daily streak. The Verify, My minutes and Public timeline sections are previews with sample data and do not write to the blockchain yet.

How do I stay safe?

Only trust links from this site, our Discord and our X account. Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone, including anyone who claims to be the team.
