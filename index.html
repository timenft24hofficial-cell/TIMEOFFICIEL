About the collection

A day has exactly 1,440 minutes. Each one is a token.

Math-Backed Scarcity

Because a day can never exceed 1,440 minutes, the total supply is fixed and final, written into the smart contract. No additional tokens will ever be released for the collection.

Dynamic Render Utility

These tokens are more than static images or clips. They update their visual state and details dynamically based on real time (UTC), working as live clocks that run directly on-chain.

Personal Emotional Anchor

Every collector can own the minute of their birth, a financial milestone, or a memory that matters to them, placed on the 24-hour timeline of the day.

Solana & MagicBlock

Built on Solana with MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollups, users get real-time interaction (under 10 ms) and can record daily moments and messages with no gas fees, while keeping full security and instant synchronization.
